CH340 (Importance)  ESP32 uses CH340 to download codes. So before using it, we need to install CH340 driver in our computers. Windows  Check whether CH340 has been installed Connect your computer and ESP32 with a USB cable. Turn to the main interface of your computer, select “This PC” and right-click to select “Manage”. Click “Device Manager”. If your computer has installed CH340, you can see”USB-SERIAL CH340 (COMx)”. And you can click here to move to the next step. Installing CH340  First, download CH340 driver, click http://www.wch-ic.com/search?q=CH340&t=downloads to download the appropriate one based on your operating system.
