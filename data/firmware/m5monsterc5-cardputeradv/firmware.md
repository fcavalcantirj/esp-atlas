M5MonsterC5-CardputerADV is an add-on board built around the M5MonsterC5 platform and based on JanOS from the Project Zero repository. It is intended for M5Stack Cardputer AVD and Tab5 devices, and it can also be used with Android and PC/MacOS over a USB connection.
