for only stickc plus2

A game for the M5StickC Plus2 where players raise strange arthropods. Players care for their pets through three actions: feeding, playing games, and cleaning. Depending on the frequency of these actions, Seamonsue develops different characteristics.
Starting from version 1.0.2, this game can also be used as a clock.
While in clock display mode, try pressing the A and B buttons simultaneously. You can set the time by tilting the device.
