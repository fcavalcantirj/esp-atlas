# TinyS2

Unexpected Maker's tiny ESP32-S2 development board, built around the ESP32-S2FN4R2 SoC with native USB and no Bluetooth. Its dedicated product page has since been retired; specs here come from Unexpected Maker's TinyS3 comparison matrix, which documents the TinyS2 as a reference point.
