# TinyPICO Nano

A smaller, castellated-header variant of the original TinyPICO, built around the ESP32-PICO-D4 SoC. Superseded by the NanoS3; specs here come from Unexpected Maker's NanoS3 comparison matrix, which documents the TinyPICO Nano as a reference point.
